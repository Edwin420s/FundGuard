## Slide 2: The Problem

### Prize distribution is a trust problem hiding inside an operations workflow.

1. **Opaque Decisions**
   - Winners and sponsors struggle to see how awards were calculated or why remaining funds remain unpaid.
2. **Manual Coordination**
   - Spreadsheets, chat messages, and disconnected banking or crypto tools cause delays, reconciliation overhead, and human error.
3. **Weak Verification**
   - A screenshot or organizer message is not independently checkable payment evidence.
4. **Rounding & Reserve Discrepancies**
   - Poorly specified allocation rules cause rounding discrepancies, unaccounted leftover balances, and contentious disputes.

> **The Cost**: Reputational damage, prolonged close-out cycles, sponsor fatigue, and operational friction for every stakeholder.
